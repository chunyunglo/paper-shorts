## Strategy 經營策略 (written by Claude)

Written 2026-10-27

- Only one paper/topic has been published so far ('AI Wrote the Petitions. Did They Win More?'), posted in 3 languages across 3 platforms, which gives 9 data points that all share the same content, so no topic comparison is possible yet.
- Instagram is clearly the strongest platform: 141 total views (55 zh, 53 en, 33 pt, average 47) versus 37 on YouTube (average 12) and 19 on Threads (average 6).
- On YouTube the Chinese version got 33 of the 37 Shorts views, the best retention (32% average watched vs 19% en and 12% pt), and the channel's only new subscriber (+1). On Threads, zh also led with 15 views vs 4 en and 0 pt.
- On Instagram, English (53) nearly matched Chinese (55), so the zh lead on YouTube and Threads may reflect early distribution luck rather than audience preference.
- Every Instagram and Threads post shows exactly 1 like and 1 comment, including a Threads post with 0 views. This looks like self or team engagement and should not be read as organic interest.
- YouTube retention is low across the board (12–32% of a ~1-minute short), so most viewers leave within the first 10–20 seconds.

**Shorts:** Keep publishing all three languages for now, and make Instagram the priority platform, since it is the only one where every language reached an audience. Lean slightly into Chinese on YouTube, where it drove nearly all views, the best retention and the only subscriber. Because retention drops fast, open with the paper's concrete finding or the question it answers in the first 3 seconds (e.g., state whether AI-written petitions actually won more). Keep titles as honest questions or results that match the paper. Above all, cover several different papers and topics so you can learn which subjects resonate, because right now there is only one topic to judge.

**Podcast:** There is no podcast data yet, so pick papers on the strength of the science and their relevance to everyday life. AI-and-civic-life topics like the petitions paper are a reasonable anchor, since that is the only topic with any traction. Have Kai and Anne state each paper's core finding early, then use the banter to unpack methods and caveats. Clip 30–60 second segments as Instagram Reels, since Instagram is currently the best-performing platform. Revisit the format once a few episodes have real view and retention numbers.

_With one topic, 9 posts and 44 total YouTube views (plus engagement that looks self-generated), these numbers are essentially noise, so treat everything above as a starting hypothesis, not a trend._
