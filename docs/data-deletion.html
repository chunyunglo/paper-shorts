Data Deletion Instructions

Behavior Decoded (行為解碼) · Last updated 2026-10-26

We keep almost nothing: public posts found by search are not stored, and for comments on our own posts we keep only an internal id that marks a comment as handled.

To request deletion

  1. Send a direct message to @human__behavior__decode on Threads or Instagram with the words "delete my data" and, if you can, a link to the comment or post.
  2. We remove any stored id linked to you and any reply we posted to you that you want removed, and any forwarded copy, within 30 days, and confirm by message.

You can also delete your own comments at any time on Threads, Instagram or YouTube; our software keeps no copy of their text (a comment forwarded privately to the owner for a personal reply stays there until deleted on request).

想刪除資料，請私訊 Threads 或 Instagram 的 @human__behavior__decode，寫「刪除我的資料」並附上留言連結。我們會在 30 天內刪除相關紀錄並回覆確認。
