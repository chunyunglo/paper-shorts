Privacy Policy

Behavior Decoded (行為解碼) · Last updated 2026-10-29

Behavior Decoded is a small educational channel run by one person. Our software publishes our own videos and helps us answer comments on our own posts on Threads, Instagram and YouTube, and leaves at most one comment a day under a related public post by someone else. This page explains what data it touches.

What we access

  • Comments on our own posts: the comment text, so we can reply to questions, hide spam, or forward a comment privately to the channel owner (by email or a private notes inbox) for a personal reply.
  • Public Threads posts found by keyword search: the post text and link, used when the owner runs the suggestion tool, to suggest posts where a short summary of research could help. The channel owner reads these and decides by hand whether to reply.
  • Public Threads posts and YouTube videos found by search: the post text or video title and description, and the link, so our software can leave at most one short comment a day, from our own account, under one post where a research finding adds to the conversation. The post we commented on is sent privately to the channel owner. We keep only an internal hashed id so we never comment on the same post or person twice.

How we use it

Only to run our own channel: replying, moderating, the private suggestion email to the owner, and the one daily comment. Text may be processed by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to draft replies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or publish this data.
  • We do not use it for advertising, profiling or tracking.
  • We do not keep search results: they are used to build one email or pick one post and then discarded. For comments on our posts we keep only an internal id marking a comment as handled, not its text.

Contact and deletion

To ask a question or have anything about you removed, see Data deletion, or send a direct message to @human__behavior__decode on Threads or Instagram.
