Privacy Policy

Behavior Decoded (行為解碼) · Last updated 2026-10-26

Behavior Decoded is a small educational channel run by one person. Our software publishes our own videos and helps us answer comments on our own posts on Threads, Instagram and YouTube. This page explains what data it touches.

What we access

  • Comments on our own posts: the comment text, so we can reply to questions, hide spam, or forward a comment to the channel owner for a personal reply.
  • Public Threads posts found by keyword search: the post text and link, used once a day to suggest posts where a short summary of research could help. The channel owner reads these and decides by hand whether to reply. Nothing is posted automatically.

How we use it

Only to run our own channel: replying, moderating, and the private daily suggestion email to the owner. Text may be processed by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to draft replies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or publish this data.
  • We do not use it for advertising, profiling or tracking.
  • We do not keep search results: they are used to build one email and then discarded. For comments on our posts we keep only an internal id marking a comment as handled, not its text.

Contact and deletion

To ask a question or have anything about you removed, see Data deletion, or send a direct message to @human__behavior__decode on Threads or Instagram.
